Privacy Policy for KiddoLearn

Last updated: October 16, 2026

KiddoLearn ("the App", "we", "us") is a children's learning app developed in Gujarat, India. This policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

KiddoLearn does not collect, store, use or share any personal information. The App does not require an account or sign-in, and it does not ask for names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera access, or any other personal data.

2. Usage Data and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, advertising IDs, analytics or any tracking technologies. We do not collect device identifiers or IP addresses.

3. Children's Privacy

KiddoLearn is designed for children. Because we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children, there is no child data to share or disclose. The App contains no third-party advertising and no links that take children outside the App.

4. Data Stored on Your Device

Learning progress and settings (such as stars earned) are stored only on your device. This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the App removes it.

5. Third-Party Services

The App does not use third-party services that collect data.

6. Security

Because we do not collect personal data, no personal data is held on our servers.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Contact Us
